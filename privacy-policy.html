00 — Overview
Our Commitment to Your Privacy

Full Professional Websites ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Plain English: We collect only what we absolutely need to build and deliver your website. We do not sell your data. We do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes. Ever.

01 — Information We Collect
What We Collect & Why

We collect the following information from clients who purchase or inquire about our services:

  • Full Name — to identify you and personalize communications
  • Phone Number — to send you your demo website link and communicate about your project via SMS
  • Email Address — to send order confirmations and project-related communications
  • Payment Method — processed securely through Stripe; we do not store raw payment card data on our servers
  • Google Maps Link — to accurately research and build your business's website using publicly available information from your business listing

We do not collect Social Security numbers, government ID, banking credentials, or any other sensitive personal information beyond what is listed above.

02 — How We Use Your Information
Use of Data

The information we collect is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Building and delivering your personalized website
  • Sending your demo link and payment link via SMS
  • Communicating about your project (follow-ups, updates, support)
  • Processing your one-time payment through Stripe
  • Providing customer support via phone or email

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing to third parties, or any purpose unrelated to fulfilling your website order.

03 — SMS Messaging
Text Message Communications

By providing your phone number and verbally consenting during a call with one of our representatives, you agree to receive SMS messages from Full Professional Websites, including:

  • Your free demo website link
  • A payment link for your order
  • Follow-up messages if you have not responded
  • Support information including our contact number: (401) 300-0957

Message frequency varies by project status. Standard message and data rates may apply. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. For support, reply HELP or call (401) 300-0957.

04 — Data Sharing
Who We Share Data With

We share your information only with the services required to operate our business:

  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe's privacy policy governs how they handle payment data.
  • Twilio — SMS delivery. Twilio is used to send demo and payment links to your phone number.
  • Vercel — website hosting for the sites we build and deliver to clients.

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise disclose your personal information to any other third party.

05 — Data Retention
How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain your information only as long as necessary to fulfill your order and provide support. If you request deletion of your data, we will remove it from our records within 30 days, unless retention is required by law (e.g., payment records).

06 — Your Rights
Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at fullprofessionalwebsites@outlook.com or call (401) 300-0957.
